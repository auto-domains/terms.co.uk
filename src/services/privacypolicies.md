## About PrivacyPolicies.com

PrivacyPolicies.com offers generators for a privacy policy, terms and conditions, a cookie policy, an EULA, a return and refund policy and a disclaimer, as well as a free cookie consent banner. It says it can produce a customised privacy policy in around three minutes and adapts each policy to the privacy laws relevant to where you operate.

The site names CPRA, CCPA and CalOPPA for businesses collecting personal information in the US, GDPR for businesses in or serving the EU, and the ePrivacy Directive for its cookie policy generator. The pages checked do not mention UK specific law.

Each generator has a free option and a premium option. Free documents include a hosted page, revisions applied to the hosted page, and downloads in HTML, DOCX and plain text. Premium clauses are priced individually as one-time fees. At the time of checking, privacy policy clauses for analytics tools cost up to $14 each, ad network clauses $7 each, payment processor clauses $9 for all, and remarketing clauses up to $12 each. GDPR, CPRA, CCPA and CalOPPA clauses were listed as premium options without a stated price.

## How to create a policy with PrivacyPolicies.com

1. Choose the generator you need and start a free or premium version.
2. Answer the questions about your business, industry and where you operate.
3. Add any premium clauses you need and pay the one-time fee.
4. Link to the free hosted page or download the document as HTML, DOCX or text.

## Things to know

- **Law clauses may cost extra:** GDPR and US state law clauses sit in the premium column, so a fully free policy may not include them.
- **No recurring fees:** Premium options are one-off payments.
- **No automatic updates described:** Revisions you make are applied to your hosted page, but the site does not describe automatic changes when laws change.
- **Check the output:** Review the policy against how your business collects and uses data, especially if you serve UK customers.
