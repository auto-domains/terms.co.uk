## About GetTerms

GetTerms is a privacy compliance tool that combines a legal policy generator with a cookie banner and consent management platform. It says its policies are drafted by lawyers and are available in several languages, and it names GDPR and UK GDPR, CCPA and CalOPPA, PIPEDA, the Australian Privacy Principles and COPPA among the laws its paid tiers help with.

Plans are priced per website. At the time of checking, Starter, which includes a privacy policy and cookie policy, was $5 a month billed annually or $9 monthly. Business, which includes all legal policies, unlimited edits and regulation monitoring, was $8 a month billed annually or $12 monthly. Lifetime licences were listed at $199 for Starter and $249 for Business. A Partner plan for agencies is priced on request. GetTerms advertises a money back guarantee.

Policies are hosted in the GetTerms app so you can link to them, and they can also be embedded as HTML, added through a WordPress plugin with shortcodes, or copied into a content editor. The comparison table also lists Markdown and PDF.

## How to create a policy with GetTerms

1. Choose a plan, or start with the free branded version.
2. Answer the questions about your website and how it handles data.
3. Review the generated policies and make any edits your plan allows.
4. Link to the hosted version, embed it as HTML or use the WordPress plugin.

## Things to know

- **Free version branding:** The free version shows GetTerms branding.
- **Which tier updates:** Ongoing updates are mentioned generally, but regulation monitoring is listed against the Business plan, so check the plan table before buying.
- **Currency:** Prices are shown with a $ sign without naming the currency.
- **Check the output:** Generated policies depend on your answers. Review them against your own business before publishing.
