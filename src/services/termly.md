## About Termly

Termly is a compliance platform that combines policy generators with a cookie consent banner and cookie scanner. Its privacy policy generator asks guided questions about your business, shows a live preview as you answer, and then lets you publish the finished policy to your website or app.

Alongside the privacy policy generator, Termly lists generators for terms and conditions, cookie policies, EULAs, acceptable use policies, disclaimers, refund and return policies, shipping policies, an Impressum and an accessibility statement. Termly says its platform covers 28 data privacy laws, including GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA and CalOPPA.

Plans are priced per website. At the time of checking, Starter was $10 a month billed annually ($14 monthly) with two policies and 10 policy edits, and Pro+ was $15 a month billed annually ($20 monthly) with unlimited policies, auto-updated policies and hosted policies. An Agency plan is priced on request. A 30 day money-back guarantee is offered.

## How to create a policy with Termly

1. Choose the generator for the document you need, for example the privacy policy generator.
2. Answer the guided questions about your business, using the tooltips and live preview.
3. Review the finished policy.
4. Click Publish and add the policy to your site as embedded HTML, or as a hosted link on Pro+.

## Things to know

- **Free plan limits:** One basic policy, no edits and no regulation monitoring. Editing a policy after publishing needs a paid plan.
- **Hosted versus embedded:** Static HTML embeds are on every plan, but dynamic hosted policies that change without you updating your site are Pro+ and Agency features.
- **Prices in dollars:** Prices are shown with a $ sign and Termly also advertises promotional codes, so check the current price at checkout.
- **Check the output:** A generated policy reflects only the answers you give. Review it against how your business actually collects and uses data, and take legal advice if your situation is unusual.
