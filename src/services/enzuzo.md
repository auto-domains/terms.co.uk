## About Enzuzo

Enzuzo is a data privacy compliance platform that combines a policy generator with a cookie consent banner and data subject access request (DSAR) forms. Its privacy policy generator asks about your business, where your customers are and how you collect data, then produces a policy you can embed on your site. Policies are offered in more than 25 languages.

Enzuzo names GDPR, CCPA, CalOPPA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, Brazil's LGPD and Saudi Arabia's PDPL. The pages checked do not mention UK GDPR. Enzuzo says policies auto-update when it detects changes to data privacy laws, and that you can edit sections in your dashboard.

At the time of checking, the free plan included a standard privacy policy and terms of service for one domain. Starter, at $7 a month billed yearly or $9 monthly, added custom privacy policies, terms of service, EULA, and shipping and returns policies. Growth was $22 a month billed yearly and Pro $59 a month billed yearly, with higher visitor and domain limits. The pricing page shows prices in US dollars.

## How to create a policy with Enzuzo

1. Sign up for the free plan or a paid plan.
2. Enter your business details.
3. Select where your customers are and the countries you serve, then answer the questions about data collection.
4. Generate the policy and add it to your site with the JavaScript embed, copy and paste, or a footer link.

## Things to know

- **Visitor limits:** Each plan has a monthly visitor allowance, starting at 5,000 on the free plan.
- **Free plan documents:** The free plan includes standard rather than custom policies.
- **Pricing table inconsistencies:** At the time of checking, the pricing page showed two slightly different plan tables, so confirm limits at sign-up.
- **Check the output:** Review generated policies against your own data practices, particularly if you serve UK customers.
