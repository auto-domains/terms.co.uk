## About SEQ Legal

SEQ Legal describes itself as a legal resource providing free document templates, compliance guides and regulatory analysis. It lists around two dozen free templates, many aimed at website owners: website terms and conditions, a website disclaimer, a privacy policy, a cookies policy, a copyright notice, a linking policy and a delivery policy, alongside an NDA, an EULA and a SaaS agreement.

The site does not give a company name, address or founding date on its About page. It says its content is reviewed by an editorial team, and the template pages carry a "last updated" date. The website terms template was last updated on 1 March 2026.

## How to get terms and conditions from SEQ Legal

1. Open the free legal documents section and choose Website Terms and Conditions.
2. Use the jurisdiction selector if you need something other than the default English law, or keep the default for a business in England and Wales.
3. Download the .doc file or copy the text. No account is needed.
4. Fill in the bracketed placeholders, such as your business details and the governing law clause.
5. Download the privacy policy and cookies policy as well if your site collects personal data or uses cookies.

## Things to know

- **Cost:** All templates are free, and no paid tier is advertised.
- **Default law:** The website terms default to English law and the courts of England and Wales. Both are bracketed placeholders that you should confirm or change.
- **Attribution:** Downloads end with an SEQ Legal credit line. Check SEQ Legal's terms on whether it may be removed.
- **Content of the terms:** The website terms cover a licence to use the site, acceptable use, user content, limited warranties, limitation of liability, variation and law and jurisdiction.
- **Not legal advice:** SEQ Legal says its templates should not be relied on as a substitute for professional legal advice. A template needs tailoring to your own business.
