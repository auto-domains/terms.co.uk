## About Wonder.Legal

Wonder.Legal is an online legal document service operated by SASU MIRACLE, a company based in Paris. It started in 2014 and says it operates in 26 countries. Its UK site lists around 180 templates across business and personal categories, with template names referring to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. It says it employs lawyers and legal experts to create and maintain its templates in each country.

For website owners, the UK site offers Website Terms and Conditions of Use, Website Terms and Conditions of Sale and a Website Privacy Policy. The privacy policy refers to the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and its page showed a last updated date of 19 July 2026.

## How to get terms and conditions from Wonder.Legal

1. Choose the template on the UK site, for example Website Terms and Conditions of Use.
2. Click next to see the price before you start, if you want to check it first.
3. Answer the questions. The document is built as you go.
4. Pay the one-off price shown at the end of the form.
5. Download the document in Word and PDF, then edit the Word version if needed.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** Prices are per document, from £1.99 to £79.99. The UK FAQ does not mention VAT or a subscription.
- **Operator details:** The terms of sale name SASU MIRACLE in Paris as the operator. The same terms cover several countries, including optional subscriptions in some of them, so read them before buying.
- **No human review:** Documents are generated by software from lawyer written templates.
- **Choose the right document:** Use terms of use for an information site and terms of sale if you take orders online, and pair either with a privacy policy if you collect personal data.
- **Not legal advice:** Check the generated document reflects how your website actually operates.
