## About Legalo

Legalo Ltd sells legal document templates for UK startups, founders and small businesses, covering business contracts and some personal documents. It says it has been helping customers since 2015 and that its documents are drafted under the laws of England and Wales by practising solicitors. Each template comes with a plain English guide, and Legalo refers customers who need bespoke advice to a law firm.

Its Ecommerce and Internet range includes website terms and conditions for selling goods to consumers, website terms of use, a website privacy policy, a cookies policy, website and email disclaimers, and documents for handling copied content. A separate range covers terms and conditions of trade for businesses that do not sell online.

## How to get terms and conditions from Legalo

1. Decide whether you need terms of use for an information site or terms and conditions of sale for an online shop.
2. Add the template to your basket on legalo.co.uk, or download the free Startup and SME Compliance Pack if it covers what you need.
3. Download the Word document.
4. Work through the clause by clause guide, deleting clauses that do not apply and filling in details such as payment terms and warranties.
5. Publish the terms and privacy policy and link to them from your site.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** Prices on the Ecommerce and Internet category page are £24.95 for website terms and conditions, £19.95 for terms of use, £34.95 for the privacy policy and £14.95 for the cookies policy. VAT treatment is not stated.
- **Price differences:** The privacy policy page shows £34.95 at the top but £29.95 in its FAQ, so check the basket price.
- **Scope:** The website terms and conditions are for selling goods to consumers and are not designed for services beyond delivery. Separate templates cover goods and services and business customers.
- **Cookies:** The privacy policy includes a cookies policy at no extra cost.
- **Guarantee:** Legalo advertises a money back guarantee.
- **Check dates:** The website terms of sale page shows a last updated date of May 2016, while the privacy policy was updated in February 2026. Check older templates against current consumer law before use.
