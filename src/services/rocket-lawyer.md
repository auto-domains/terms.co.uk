## About Rocket Lawyer

Rocket Lawyer's UK site is run by Rocket Lawyer UK Limited, a company registered in England and Wales. It combines a library of legal document templates with access to lawyers, offered through a monthly membership. The company itself is not a law firm, but its terms say the lawyers it works with, including Rocket Lawyer Solicitors, are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority.

For websites, it offers website terms and conditions, a privacy policy and a cookie policy, along with terms and conditions for selling goods or supplying services online and AI terms of service for software products. The website terms template covers acceptable and prohibited use, registration and passwords, linked websites, site availability, disclaimers and limitation of liability.

## How to get terms and conditions from Rocket Lawyer

1. Open the document page, for example Website Terms and Conditions, and choose to make the document.
2. Answer the interview questions. Rocket Lawyer asks for your business details, website address, contact email, any registration or fee details, and the addresses of your privacy and cookie policies if you have them.
3. Sign up for an account or start the 7 day free trial. The finished document is saved to your Rocket Lawyer dashboard.
4. Download and publish it, and keep a copy of each version you use.
5. If you want a second opinion, members can ask a lawyer a question or request a document review.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** Membership is £34.99 per month after the 7 day free trial. The pricing page does not say whether this includes VAT. Cancellation takes effect at the next renewal date.
- **Refunds:** Its terms say no refund is available once you have downloaded a document.
- **Jurisdiction:** The website terms default to the law of England and Wales. The privacy policy page refers to the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.
- **Not legal advice:** Rocket Lawyer describes its documents as customisable templates and a starting point, and does not guarantee its legal information is current. Check the finished terms match how your site actually works.
- **Use the UK pages:** Rocket Lawyer also runs a US site. Make sure you start from the GB pages if your business is in the UK.
