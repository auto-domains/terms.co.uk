## About Genie AI

Genie AI is a legal drafting platform operated by Genie AI Ltd. It combines a library of more than 500 templates with AI tools for drafting and reviewing contracts, and a Word style editor. Its UK pages offer templates labelled for England and Wales, including a terms of use agreement, UK website terms and conditions, terms and conditions of sale, and several privacy documents such as a website privacy policy, a privacy notice and a UK GDPR privacy notice.

The terms of use template is described as covering user rights and obligations, liability limits, data protection and intellectual property under English and Welsh law, with reference to the Consumer Rights Act 2015, UK GDPR and the E-Commerce Regulations.

## How to get terms and conditions from Genie AI

1. Open the UK template page, for example Terms of Use Agreement or Website Privacy Policy.
2. Download the template, or choose a governing law and enter your key requirements to generate a tailored version.
3. Sign up for the Free plan if you want to use the AI drafting tools or editor.
4. Review the draft and edit it to match how your website and business work.
5. Upgrade to a paid plan if you need unlimited exports or more AI usage.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** The Free plan costs nothing and needs no card. Pro is $59 per month billed annually or $75 per month, and Business is $259 per month billed annually or $320 per month. Prices are shown in US dollars and VAT treatment is not stated.
- **Free plan limits:** The pricing page says Free plan exports are locked until you upgrade, while template pages describe the documents as free to use. Check before you start drafting.
- **Page content:** The UK website terms and conditions page shows sample clauses for a terms of sale document, so make sure the file you download is the type you need.
- **Review:** Templates are reviewed by Genie AI's legal engineers. The upload and review feature is automated, not a human lawyer review.
- **Not legal advice:** AI generated drafts still need checking against your own business and data practices.
