## About Net Lawman

Net Lawman Limited is an English company based in Suffolk that started trading in 2001. It sells legal document templates for business, property and personal use, with over 450 documents in its UK range. It says its managing director, a solicitor, has drawn or approved every document it sells.

Rather than a single set of website terms, Net Lawman offers versions for different kinds of site, including membership sites, SaaS and on demand IT services, software download sites, marketplaces, hotels and bed and breakfasts, and businesses selling goods internationally to other businesses. It also publishes free website privacy policy templates, including versions for legal services providers, property agents and accountants.

## How to get terms and conditions from Net Lawman

1. Browse the website terms range and pick the version that matches how your site trades. Net Lawman invites you to contact it if you are unsure which to choose.
2. Choose a purchase option: document only, Lawyer Assist, bespoke drafting or a membership plan.
3. Download the template in Word, Apple Pages or RTF format.
4. Edit it using the guidance notes at the end of the document. Document only buyers can email questions about editing.
5. If you bought Lawyer Assist, send your edited draft for a one to one review before finalising.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** Document only purchases range from free to £95, Lawyer Assist from £84 to £240 and bespoke drafting from £500. Product pages show Lawyer Assist and bespoke prices including VAT.
- **Membership:** Standard is £95, Professional £195 and Professional Plus £375 per year, giving 10 or 25 library downloads plus Lawyer Assist discounts. Plans do not auto renew.
- **Guarantee:** Product pages offer a refund if the document is not right for your circumstances.
- **Updates:** Net Lawman says its lawyers regularly review how new law affects its library. Keep your own published terms under review as the law changes.
- **Check the fit:** Some templates are business to business only. If you sell to consumers, choose a version that covers consumer cancellation rights.
