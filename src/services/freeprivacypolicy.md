## About FreePrivacyPolicy.com

FreePrivacyPolicy.com is a self-serve site offering free generators for a privacy policy, terms and conditions, a cookie policy, a return and refund policy, a disclaimer and an EULA, plus a free cookie consent banner. It says it has helped more than a million site owners create policies.

The privacy policy generator says it includes provisions for the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA and CPRA), CalOPPA and the GDPR, as well as third party requirements from Google Play, the Apple App Store, Google AdSense, Google Ads and Google Analytics. The pages checked do not mention UK specific law.

Generated policies are saved to your account, where you can download, update or edit them later. The site provides free hosting so you can link to your policy from any website or app, or you can copy the HTML code into your own page. A premium version with more provisions and clauses is available, but the price was not shown on the generator pages checked.

## How to create a policy with FreePrivacyPolicy.com

1. Choose the generator for the document you need.
2. Answer the questions about your business and your website or app.
3. Log in to your account to download the policy or copy its HTML.
4. Link to the free hosted version or paste the HTML into your site, and log back in to update it later.

## Things to know

- **Premium pricing not shown:** The generator pages offer a premium version but do not list its price before you start.
- **Payments:** The site footer says purchases are sold and fulfilled by FastSpring as an authorised reseller.
- **No automatic updates described:** The site explains how to log in and update a policy yourself.
- **Check the output:** A free generated policy is a starting point. Make sure it reflects how your business handles personal data, including any UK GDPR obligations.
