## About ContractStore

The ContractStore Limited is a private company incorporated in England, with a registered office in Richmond, Surrey. It publishes a library of more than 250 contract and document templates for businesses, supplied as editable Microsoft Word files with explanatory notes. Prices can be shown in pounds, euros, Australian dollars or US dollars, and the site also has sections for other jurisdictions such as Ireland and the USA.

Its Internet and E-commerce range includes Ecommerce Terms and Conditions for a shopping website, a Privacy Notice template, Website and Email Legal Notices, a Data Protection Policy, a data processing contract and a Web Design Contract. The ecommerce terms cover ordering online, price, payment, delivery, consumer cancellation rights, risk and ownership, warranties and liability, intellectual property and governing law.

## How to get terms and conditions from ContractStore

1. Open the Internet and E-commerce section and read the free explanatory notes for the template you are considering.
2. Add the document to your basket. Buying three or more ContractStore documents together gives a 15% discount.
3. Pay, then download the Word file from the order confirmation email or your account area.
4. Edit the template to reflect your business, prices, delivery arrangements and returns process.
5. Add a privacy notice and legal notices if your site collects personal data.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** The ecommerce terms product page shows £59 + VAT. The category page lists the Privacy Notice at £15 and Website and Email Legal Notices at £20.50 without stating VAT treatment.
- **Price differences:** Some ContractStore pages have shown the ecommerce terms at £39, so check the price in your basket.
- **Updates:** The ecommerce terms page showed a last modified date of 31 August 2026.
- **Reuse:** ContractStore says you can edit a template and use it on more than one project.
- **Governing law:** The ecommerce terms include a governing law clause, but the product page does not name which law it selects. Check it suits where you trade.
- **Not legal advice:** A template is a starting point and needs tailoring to your own business.
