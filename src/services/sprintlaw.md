## About Sprintlaw UK

Sprintlaw (UK) Ltd, trading as Sprintlaw, is based in London and describes itself as a legal consultancy rather than a law firm. Unlike a pure template library, its main service is having a lawyer draft documents tailored to your business for a fixed fee. For websites and online businesses it offers website terms of use, online service terms and conditions, online goods and services terms, subscription terms, app terms and privacy policies, as well as a startup privacy and terms starter pack.

It also runs an online platform with a library of lawyer vetted templates, contract storage, electronic signing and an AI legal assistant called Taylor. Templates on the platform, including web terms, are bought with credits.

## How to get terms and conditions from Sprintlaw UK

1. Submit an enquiry on the relevant package page, such as Website Terms of Use.
2. A legal project manager reviews it within one business day and sends a fixed fee quote setting out cost, scope and timing.
3. Accept and e-sign the engagement letter online.
4. Speak with a lawyer by phone or video about your business and website.
5. Receive the finished document in Word or PDF, usually within 5 working days, with one complimentary amendment.

## Things to know

- **Pricing:** Website terms are quoted individually. Expenses and disbursements are not included in the fixed fee, and work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately.
- **Platform plans:** The platform pricing page lists a Free plan and a Plus plan at £399 per year with unlimited lawyer calls and £125 of credits each quarter. Other pages refer to a standard membership from £33 per month, so check the current plans. VAT treatment is not stated.
- **Regulation:** Sprintlaw is not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, which may affect the protections available to you.
- **Best suited to:** Businesses that want terms drafted around their own model rather than editing a template themselves.
