## What this site is

{{ site.domain }} lists online policy generators and legal template sites that help you create terms and conditions, terms of service, privacy policies, cookie policies, refund policies and similar documents. Each listing summarises what the service produces, what it costs, whether there is a free option, which laws it says it covers and whether it hosts and updates your documents for you.

The aim is to help website owners, online shops, app developers and small businesses compare the options quickly before choosing one.

## How listings are researched

Each service page is written from the service's own website, including its pricing, product pages, FAQ and terms. Prices and features are only included when the service publishes them, and every page lists its sources and the date it was last checked.

Services change their prices and plans often. If you spot something out of date, please [get in touch](/contact/).

## Not legal advice

{{ site.domain }} does not give legal advice and does not review the documents any service produces. Whether a generated or template document is right for you depends on your business. If you are unsure, take advice from a qualified solicitor.

## Independence

{{ site.domain }} is not connected to any of the services listed. Listings are not paid for, and links to services are ordinary links that do not earn a commission.
