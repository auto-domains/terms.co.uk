## Before you get in touch

{{ site.domain }} is a directory. **It does not create legal documents or give legal advice**, so it cannot:

- write or review terms and conditions or a privacy policy for you
- help with an account, subscription or refund on another service
- answer questions about a document a service has produced

For these, contact the service you used, or a solicitor for legal advice. Each service page in this directory links to the service's own website. Start from the [list of services](/).
