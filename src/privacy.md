This policy explains how {{ site.domain }} ("this website", "we") collects and uses information when you visit. It was last updated on {{ updated | readableDate }}.

## Who we are

{{ site.domain }} is an independent directory of terms and conditions generators and legal template sites. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## Information we collect

This website does not have user accounts. If you use the domain enquiry form, the details you enter are sent to Zoho Forms, which we use to receive and manage enquiries. We may process:

- **Messages and enquiries you send us.** If you email us or use the domain enquiry form, we use your contact details and message to reply and keep a record of the correspondence.
- **Technical data.** When you visit, basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request is processed by our hosting provider to deliver the website and protect it from abuse.
- **Analytics and advertising data.** With your consent, cookies and similar technologies collect information about how you use the website and help show adverts, as described below.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes technical information about requests to the website in order to deliver pages quickly and securely. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This website does not use cookies of its own for essential functions. Third-party services we use may set cookies if you agree to them.

### Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed most often. This helps us improve the directory. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages visited, approximate location and device type. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

### Google advertising

This website may show adverts provided by Google, including through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve adverts based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve adverts to you based on your visits to this website and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). You can find out more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your consent choices

Where required by law, including for visitors in the UK and European Economic Area, analytics and advertising cookies are only used after you give consent through the consent message shown when you first visit. Until you make a choice, Google services are told not to store cookies for analytics or advertising. You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer of each page, where available, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

## Legal basis

We rely on your **consent** for analytics and advertising cookies. We rely on our **legitimate interests** in running a secure and reliable website for processing technical data, and in responding to messages you send us.

## How long information is kept

Emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards. Analytics data is retained in line with the retention settings in Google Analytics. Cookies set by Google remain on your device for the period set by Google unless you delete them.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

If you are unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

These websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.
